Product

One camera. A complete picture of your baby's world.

Image sensor IR night vision Microphone array Environmental sensors

  • Camera system

    High-resolution image sensor and wide-angle optics, designed for a full view of the crib from a bedside or wall mount.

  • Night vision

    Infrared illumination and low-light processing for clear video in a dark nursery, without waking your baby.

  • Microphone array

    Multiple microphones for directional sound capture and robust cry and sound classification.

  • Local processing

    An on-device neural processor runs vision and audio models at the edge, so events are recognized in milliseconds.

  • Environmental sensors

    Temperature, humidity and ambient light, correlated with sleep to explain the "why" behind restless nights.

  • Secure connectivity

    Encrypted Wi‑Fi transport and authenticated device identity, designed so only your family can see your feed.

Safety & Privacy

Privacy by design.

Video of a child is among the most sensitive data a family has. These are the principles BabyWatch is built on.

  • Encrypted transmission

    Video and events are designed to travel over encrypted connections, end to end.

  • Edge-first processing

    Vision and audio models run on the device wherever possible. Events, not raw footage, are what leave the home.

  • User-controlled sharing

    Parents decide who can view, for how long, and can revoke access at any time.

  • Secure cloud architecture

    Isolated storage, least-privilege access and audited data flows as architectural requirements.

  • Strong account security

    Designed for multi-factor authentication, device-bound sessions and clear activity history.

  • Privacy-first product design

    Data minimization by default. We collect what the intelligence needs, nothing more.

These are architecture principles and design goals. BabyWatch does not claim third-party certifications at this stage.
